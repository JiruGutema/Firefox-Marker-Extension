Welcome to the whiteboard

Your boards are saved automatically. Use the toolbar to draw, write, add shapes and sticky notes.

  • Scroll to move around, Ctrl + scroll to zoom.
  • Hold Space and drag (or drag with the middle mouse button) to pan.
  • Create more boards with + at the top left.
  • Press ? for all keyboard shortcuts, and use the save button to export PNG, SVG or PDF.
